# Delivery Policy — Demo Version

**Status:** Fictional demonstration policy; service areas, fees, and times are not real offers.

1. Delivery eligibility must be checked against the current approved service-area data.
2. Do not infer coverage just because a city appears in an example list.
3. Quote delivery fees only from an approved delivery-rate record for the customer's destination and delivery method.
4. Quote delivery estimates only when the system has a current estimate. Otherwise explain that the team needs to confirm.
5. Do not claim an order has shipped or arrived without a trusted order/carrier status.
6. Store timestamps in UTC and display customer-facing times in West Africa Time (WAT, UTC+1).
7. Escalate missing, stale, or contradictory delivery data.
