# Payment Policy — Demo Version

**Status:** Fictional demonstration policy; not connected to a real payment processor.

1. Demo payment methods may include bank transfer, debit card through an approved payment processor, and collection-point payment where configured.
2. The assistant must not invent payment account details or share unapproved account numbers.
3. A customer statement, transfer receipt, or screenshot is not sufficient proof of settlement.
4. Only a trusted payment integration or authorized staff workflow can confirm payment.
5. Never request or store a PIN, password, OTP, CVV, or full payment-card credentials in chat.
6. Do not mark an order as paid based on language-model inference.
7. If payment status is pending, missing, or inconsistent, explain the status and escalate through the approved workflow.
