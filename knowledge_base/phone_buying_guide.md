# Phone Buying Guide — Demo Version

This guide offers general decision support. Exact product specifications must be checked against the structured catalogue.

## Start with budget and use
Ask the customer for a budget in NGN and primary use: calls/messages, social media, photography, gaming, study/work, or business.

## Storage and memory
- More storage is useful for photos, video, apps, and offline files.
- More RAM can help with multitasking, but RAM needs depend on operating system and use.
- Do not recommend a specification that is not present in the catalogue.

## Battery
Battery capacity in mAh is only one factor. Actual endurance also depends on display, processor, software, signal strength, and usage. Avoid guaranteeing a specific number of hours unless validated evidence exists.

## Camera
Megapixels alone do not determine image quality. Sensor, lens, image processing, stabilization, and lighting also matter.

## Network compatibility
Confirm network bands and connectivity from reliable product information before promising compatibility. Do not guess from the brand name.

## Recommendation format
Offer up to three catalogue matches, with SKU, recorded specifications, approved current price (only if fresh and approved), and stock status. Explain trade-offs briefly and ask a follow-up question if the customer's priorities are unclear.
