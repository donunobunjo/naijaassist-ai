# Warranty Policy — Demo Version

**Status:** Fictional portfolio demonstration policy. Not a real retailer's warranty or legal advice.

1. Warranty duration, if any, is taken from the approved product record and purchase record.
2. Warranty starts from the confirmed purchase/delivery date specified by the final approved terms.
3. Proof of purchase may be required for an assessment.
4. Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repair, misuse, and consumable/accessory exclusions must not be assumed; the approved product-specific terms govern.
5. The assistant must not guarantee coverage or reject a claim solely from a short chat description.
6. Missing or conflicting warranty information must be escalated to a human support representative.
7. Never invent a warranty period. If the catalogue says 6 months, report that as the recorded demo term and make clear it is subject to approved terms.
