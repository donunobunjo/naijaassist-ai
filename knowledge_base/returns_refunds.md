# Returns and Refunds — Demo Version

**Status:** Fictional portfolio demonstration policy, not a universal legal rule.

1. Raise an eligible return request within seven calendar days of the recorded delivery date under this initial demo rule.
2. Do not automatically reject a complaint just because the stated period has passed. Escalate exceptions, damaged/incorrect goods, delivery disputes, and questions about statutory rights.
3. Record the order identifier, delivery date, item, reason, and relevant evidence through approved systems. Do not request unnecessary personal information.
4. A return request is not an approval. The support team must assess eligibility under the approved terms.
5. Refund timing depends on the verified payment method and confirmed processing status. Never promise a specific refund date without trusted status data.
6. Do not ask customers to disclose card PINs, passwords, OTPs, or CVVs.
7. The AI must not claim a refund has been issued unless the trusted payment/order system confirms it.
